Wonders happen when we come together

No student walks alone. We provide food, transport, manuals, healthcare and school fees support, and build student communities that come through for one another.

Who we are

Wonders Foundation is a faith-rooted aid and community foundation for students. The name honours our founder’s father, whose name was Wonders, and Isaiah 9:6 names Jesus “Wonderful”. We are Christian in foundation and open in service: every student in need is welcome, whatever their religion, tribe or background, and faith is never a condition of help.

What we do

Wonders Aid

Food, clothing, transport, data, manuals, medical care and emergency help.

Wonders Fund

A general pool that covers everyday needs and larger ones like school fees and rent.

Wonders Crowdfunds

When a need is very large or the pool is running low, we open a verified crowdfund so many people can come through for one student.

Wonders Communities

School chapters where members ask, give and come through for each other.

Why we exist

A student can have the grades, the admission and the dream, and still wonder whether there is enough for the bus home, the next handout or the meal they skipped to pay for both. No student should have to choose between eating and learning, and no student should carry that pressure alone.

No student walks alone.
God provides, we show up.

Good to know

Where does my gift go, and can I choose?

Use the “Where should it go?” menu to choose food, transport, data and manuals, clothing, healthcare, school fees or rent. If you leave it on “where it is needed most”, it joins the general pool, so it can’t be traced to one student, and the report shows what the pool achieved together.

How do I give?

Choose an amount and tap Give. We open WhatsApp so your coordinator can send you the account details. When our organisation bank account is ready, we will show it here.

How will I know my gift was used well?

Wherever possible we pay the school, hospital, vendor or provider directly and keep the receipt. The pool’s activity is also shared in a report.

Does Wonders take a share of my gift to run the foundation?

Running costs come from the general pool, so the pool supports students and also covers the costs of running Wonders. We don’t have a figure yet, and once we have enough history we will share how much goes where.

Can I give towards one student’s need?

Yes. Pick School fees or Rent in the menu, or see our open crowdfunds. When a need is very large or the pool is running low, we open a verified crowdfund for one student, with their consent.

What does giving anonymously mean?

Your name will not appear in any public list, report or thank-you. We keep your details privately only to match your gift and send your receipt.

Can I change or stop a regular gift?

Yes. Every reminder email has a link to stop. Nothing is taken from your account automatically.

How can I check that Wonders is genuine?

Use only the contact details and links on this site, and expect a receipt for every gift. Our registration details will be shown here once registration is complete.

Do I need to share your faith to give or to receive help?

No. Wonders is Christian in foundation and open to everyone. Your faith, tribe or background makes no difference to giving or to getting help.

Partner with us so no student walks alone

Individuals, churches, businesses and corporates are all partners. Someone giving ₦2,000 a month is as much a partner as anyone.

Giving or partnering?

Giving

Give any amount, once or whenever you like, straight to the general pool. No sign-up and no commitment. The pool’s activity report is shared with everyone who gives.

Best for: a gift today.

Partnering

A regular commitment: a set amount or an in-kind gift every month, or sponsoring a specific cause such as a student’s term or a category of need for a semester. A coordinator confirms the details with you, and you choose how you are recognised.

Best for: churches, businesses, corporates, and individuals who want to stand with students over time.

Either way, your gift goes through Wonders, and partners and students never contact each other. Specific causes also receive a short summary and receipt once the need is closed.
